The experience

One lap. Everything matters.

Redline distills rally driving into a focused time trial: learn the route, feel the grip change, and find time in every sector.

01

Grip changes with the road

Asphalt rewards commitment. Gravel asks for restraint. Dirt, mud, and the rock garden test how quickly you adapt your line.

02

Manual or automatic

Own the clutch and every shift, or let the gearbox work while you focus on the racing line.

03

Every run, replayed

Finish, inspect your sectors, then orbit the replay to study every braking point and slide.

04

A route built to be learned

Mountain asphalt, forest gravel, muddy quarry ruts, a water crossing, and an off-road jump form one connected circuit.
